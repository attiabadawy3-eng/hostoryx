FAQ

Does Hostoryx connect directly to Airbnb or Booking.com?

Not in the launch edition. You can enter bookings manually or import compatible CSV data. The launch edition includes a generic mapper plus an Airbnb Earnings preset; the exact fields available still depend on the report exported by the booking platform.

Will dates change language because of my Windows or browser settings?

No for Hostoryx’s app controls. Hostoryx uses its own date and month picker and formats the displayed date based on the selected Hostoryx language.

Where is my data stored?

The downloadable HTML application stores its working data locally in your browser profile. You can export a JSON backup and CSV files. Regular backups are recommended.

Is this accounting or tax software?

No. Hostoryx provides operational and management reporting. Tax and statutory accounting requirements vary by country and should be verified with a qualified professional.

Can I use it on more than one property?

Yes. The product is designed around a multi-property portfolio and includes property-level profitability and owner reports.

Is there a monthly fee?

No monthly Hostoryx fee for the downloadable launch edition.
