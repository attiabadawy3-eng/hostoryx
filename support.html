Support

Before reporting a problem

  • Export a backup if the app contains important records.
  • Confirm the Hostoryx version shown in the footer.
  • Note your browser and operating system.
  • Write down the exact steps that reproduce the issue.

Data safety

Do not send passports, identity documents, bank details, card information, or unnecessary guest data in support messages. If sample data is required to reproduce a bug, remove personal information first.

Purchase support

The customer support email appears on Lemon Squeezy receipts and invoices. Include your order reference when contacting support about a purchase.
