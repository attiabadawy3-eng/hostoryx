Privacy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Hostoryx application data

The downloadable Hostoryx Launch Edition is local-first. Guest, booking, expense, maintenance and inventory records entered into the downloadable HTML application are stored in the browser profile on the user's device. The application does not require a Hostoryx cloud account and does not transmit those operational records to Hostoryx.

Backups and exports

Users control the JSON backups and CSV exports they create. Those files should be stored securely because they may contain business or guest information supplied by the user.

Website and checkout

This marketing website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Purchases, once enabled, are processed by Lemon Squeezy. Payment, tax, receipt and merchant-verification data handled by Lemon Squeezy is subject to Lemon Squeezy's own privacy and payment-processing terms.

Email and support

If you contact Hostoryx for support, information you voluntarily include in the message is used to respond to the request. Do not email passports, identity documents, payment card data, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Contact

Support contact details are provided on the purchase receipt and Hostoryx storefront.
