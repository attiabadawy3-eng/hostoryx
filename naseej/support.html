NASEEJ Support

NASEEJ is a local offline-first laundry management system for Windows.

Before contacting support

Keep a current backup of your NASEEJ data. Note the app version, Windows version, and a short description of the issue.

Support

Use the publisher support contact available on the NASEEJ Microsoft Store listing. For licensing requests, include the device identifier shown by NASEEJ and do not send customer data unless support specifically requires it.

Privacy

See the NASEEJ Privacy Policy.
