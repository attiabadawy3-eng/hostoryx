NASEEJ Privacy Policy

Effective: October 4, 2026

NASEEJ is an offline-first laundry management application for Windows. This policy explains how information is handled by the NASEEJ application.

Information handled by the app

Depending on how a business uses NASEEJ, the app may store business information, customer names and phone numbers, invoice and payment records, service and pricing data, employee/user accounts, operational notes, and related business records entered by the user.

Where data is stored

NASEEJ is designed to store application data locally on the Windows device used by the business. NASEEJ does not automatically upload business or customer records to a NASEEJ cloud service.

Licensing and device information

NASEEJ may create a local device identifier and local licensing/trial state to validate the software license and protect the trial period. This information is used locally by the application and is not automatically transmitted by NASEEJ.

Backups and exports

The user can create backups or exports. Any copy, transfer, upload, or sharing of those files performed by the user or by third-party software is controlled by the user and is outside NASEEJ's automatic processing.

Sharing and selling data

NASEEJ does not sell customer or business data. The application does not automatically share local business records with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on the device and in backups until it is deleted by the authorized business user, removed by the business's own retention process, or removed when the application data is deleted.

Security

NASEEJ uses local access controls and application-level safeguards. Businesses remain responsible for Windows account security, device security, backups, and controlling access to exported files.

External integrations

If a business independently configures external access, remote support, third-party backup, messaging, or other integrations, those services may have their own privacy practices. NASEEJ does not enable such third-party transfer automatically.

Children

NASEEJ is business software and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when NASEEJ functionality changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the publisher support contact shown on the NASEEJ Microsoft Store listing or visit the NASEEJ Support page.
