How to measure real short-term rental profitability

Revenue is not profit. A property can look busy and still perform poorly if platform fees, cleaning, maintenance and fixed costs are not included in the same monthly view.

Start with gross booking revenue

Use the amount generated by occupied nights before subtracting tracked operating costs. Then subtract channel fees, cleaning cost, variable operating cost, maintenance and monthly fixed costs.

Track occupancy and ADR together

High occupancy can be purchased with low pricing, while high ADR can hide weak demand. Looking at both together—and adding RevPAR—gives a more useful picture of how efficiently available nights are being monetized.

Know your break-even occupancy

Break-even occupancy tells you approximately how many occupied nights are required to cover fixed and variable costs at a given rate. This is especially useful when deciding whether a discount is still commercially sensible.
