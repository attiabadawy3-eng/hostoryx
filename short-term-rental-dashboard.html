What a useful short-term rental dashboard should actually show

A dashboard is valuable only when it helps you make a decision or complete an operation faster. For independent hosts and small property managers, the most useful setup combines financial performance with daily operational work.

Core financial metrics

At minimum, track gross revenue, channel fees, cleaning cost, operating expenses, maintenance, net income, occupancy, ADR and RevPAR. Looking at revenue alone can make a property appear healthy even when margins are being eroded by channel fees and operating costs.

Daily operations

A practical dashboard should also show arrivals, departures, cleaning turnovers, unpaid bookings, maintenance items and low-stock inventory. These are the items that create missed handoffs and guest problems when they live in separate spreadsheets or message threads.

Portfolio view

For multiple properties, one calendar and one profitability view matter more than extra charts. You should be able to compare units, see cross-month stays and identify where occupancy or cost performance is drifting.
